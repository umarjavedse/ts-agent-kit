# Code of conduct

ts-agent-kit aims to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give credit for other people's work and ideas.
- Focus feedback on the code and the problem, not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so. Report problems to **umarjavedse@gmail.com**; reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
